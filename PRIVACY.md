# Privacy Policy for Comp OWL (Weightlifting Comp Warmup)

**Effective Date:** October 26, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 26, 2026  

**Developer:** Matt Cunningham (`@lifting_while_diabetic`)  
**Application Name:** Comp OWL (Weightlifting Comp Warmup)  
**Package / Bundle ID:** `com.weightlifting.compwarmup`

---

## 1. Overview

**Comp OWL** (*Competition Olympic WeightLifting Warmup*) is an open-source mobile and desktop utility for calculating and tracking Olympic weightlifting competition warmup timings and barbell plate loading.

Your privacy is important to us. **Comp OWL does not collect, transmit, rent, sell, or share any personal data, usage analytics, or device information.**

---

## 2. Information Collection and Use

- **No Personal Data Collected:** Comp OWL does not require user registration, accounts, or sign-ins, and does not collect any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or health data).
- **100% Local On-Device Storage:** All settings, lifter warmup profiles, weight jump tables, and timing preferences you create or edit within the app are stored **exclusively locally on your device** within the application's private sandbox directory. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share a profile file yourself.
- **No Analytics, Tracking, or Advertising:** Comp OWL contains no third-party analytics SDKs, crash-reporting trackers, advertising networks, or background data collection services.

---

## 3. Device Permissions and Network Access

- **Offline Operation:** Comp OWL functions completely offline and does not make background network requests.
- **External Links:** The "About" screen contains optional links to the project's public GitHub repositories and the developer's Instagram profile. Tapping these links opens your device's default web browser or app, which is governed by the respective third-party platform's privacy policy.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

Because Comp OWL does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from any user of any age, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international equivalents.
